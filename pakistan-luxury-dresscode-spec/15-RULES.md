# 15 — Engineering & Art-Direction Rules

1. Do not copy protected branding, text, logos or images from the reference.
2. Do not invent factual cultural claims; document regional craft provenance when naming a tradition.
3. Do not use animation as a substitute for information architecture.
4. Do not make desktop the only polished viewport.
5. Never rely on hover for core commerce actions.
6. Keep motion slow and intentional.
7. Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
8. Reuse existing project infrastructure when safe.
9. Keep content/data separate from layout components.
10. Test every interactive overlay with keyboard navigation.
11. Do not autoplay audio.
12. Use original imagery or properly licensed imagery.
13. Keep the palette restrained.
14. Do not cover every surface with cultural motifs.
15. Prefer tactile material photography over decorative graphics.
16. Preserve semantic HTML.
17. Treat reduced-motion as a supported experience.
18. Do not introduce fake testimonials, fake awards or unsupported claims.
