# 02 — Brand Direction

## Working brand

Use a temporary working name such as **NAVA** until the final brand name is chosen. The codebase must make the brand name a single configuration value.

## Positioning

A contemporary Pakistani luxury fashion house translating regional craft, tailoring and textile knowledge into modern silhouettes.

## Brand attributes

- quiet
- precise
- tactile
- sophisticated
- contemporary
- culturally grounded
- globally legible

## Brand voice

Short, confident, editorial.

Prefer:

- “CRAFT, REFINED.”
- “FORM WITH MEMORY.”
- “THE NEW PAKISTANI SILHOUETTE.”
- “MADE WITH INTENTION.”
- “TEXTURE BECOMES LANGUAGE.”

Avoid:

- overblown luxury clichés
- long marketing paragraphs above the fold
- “best quality” claims without evidence
- generic “shop now” repetition

## Cultural direction

Pakistan has documented craft traditions across regions. The National Heritage and Culture Division's National Register describes Ajrak as a Sindh traditional craft using block printing and natural dyes, including geometric, floral and calligraphic pattern language. Use such references thoughtfully and document the provenance of any specific textile or motif. Source: https://heritage.pakistan.gov.pk/SiteImage/Misc/files/ICH%20Pakistan%20Low.pdf

Other directions may include regional embroidery, shawl weaving and block printing, but do not flatten distinct regional traditions into one generic “Pakistani pattern.”

## Audience

- luxury ready-to-wear customers
- couture clients
- diaspora buyers
- fashion editors and stylists
- culturally interested global customers
- design-conscious local customers
